# MeteoCatch Pro Offer and Pricing

## Positioning

MeteoCatch Pro should sell planning leverage, not safety access. Current conditions, basic risk awareness, and today/tomorrow decision support must remain understandable without payment.

Core promise:

> Plan better fishing sessions across time, spots, alerts, and glanceable surfaces.

Avoid:

- Promising catches.
- Promising long-range weather certainty.
- Hiding safety-critical wind, tide, river, or regulation warnings.

## Free

Free should include:

- Current spot conditions.
- Today and near-term forecast decision card.
- Basic fish activity and best window.
- Basic tide/river and weather summary.
- One active spot/favorite workflow.
- Rising tide alert for one active spot.
- Best solunar alert for one active spot.
- Morning report for one active spot.
- Catch journal basics.

## Pro

Pro should include:

- 10-day planning comparison and future multi-day planning expansions.
- Monthly and seasonal planner when long-range tide/cycle data is available.
- Advanced alerts: wind drop, high species activity, multi-spot alert sets.
- Multiple saved planning spots and trip shortlist.
- Widgets, Live Activities, and Apple Watch surfaces.
- Journal analytics and pattern insights.
- Export/share trip plan and catch summary.

## Pricing Hypotheses

Initial hypotheses to validate:

- Monthly: 3.99 USD/EUR.
- Yearly: 24.99 USD/EUR with a 7-day trial.
- Lifetime: 89.99 to 129.99 USD/EUR only after retention validation.

Recommended launch test:

- Primary CTA: yearly trial.
- Secondary: monthly.
- No lifetime plan in the first public test unless App Store economics require it.

## Launch Copy

Use:

- `Plan your next best window.`
- `Compare upcoming days, save alert sets, and keep the window on your Lock Screen.`
- `Long-range views use cycle and tide signals where live weather is unavailable.`

Avoid:

- `Guaranteed best fishing days.`
- `Accurate monthly weather.`
- `Never miss a catch.`

## Boundaries

Safety-critical current data should not be paywalled:

- Wind/gust danger.
- High wave/current warning.
- River stress warning.
- Regulation and protected-area warning.
- Privacy controls.
- Notification permission controls.

Premium gates can appear around advanced planning depth, number of spots, alert automation, and OS surfaces.
